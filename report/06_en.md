# Experiment Report: Thermodynamic Stability Validation of AI-Discovered Novel Zirconia Materials

## 1. Objective
This experiment validates the thermodynamic stability of a novel co-doped zirconia recipe recommended by an AI model. Using empirical rules based on ionic radii, we compute the average cation radius, radius mismatch, and a tolerance-factor-like ratio to predict phase stability, and generate crystal structure files for subsequent DFT (first-principles) calculations.

## 2. Target system and inputs
* **Script**: `06_verify_stability.py`
* **Input data**: best recipe recommended by the AI model (`ai_discovery_best_recipe.csv`)
* **Baseline reference**: the code uses a composition with 8 at% Y on the cation sublattice (Zr₀.₉₂Y₀.₀₈) as the baseline (theoretical radius ~85.43 pm, ratio ~0.619).
  > ⚠️ **Note**: here “8YSZ” means 8% Y molar fraction on the **cation** sites, which is **not** the commonly used materials-science convention “8 mol% Y₂O₃-stabilized ZrO₂” (which corresponds to ~14.8% Y on the cation sites). These two definitions differ significantly in the ionic-radius baseline; be careful with the context.

## 3. Principles and methods
We evaluate stability using a physics model based on **Shannon ionic radii**, focusing on three key metrics:

1.  **Average cation radius ($r_{avg}$)**: reflects the overall lattice expansion/contraction trend.
2.  **Radius mismatch ($\delta$)**: computed as the variance of cation radii to quantify lattice distortion.
    * Formula: $\sqrt{\sum c_i (r_i - r_{avg})^2}$
3.  **Cation/anion ratio**: analogous to the Goldschmidt tolerance factor, used to infer phase stability (cubic vs. tetragonal vs. monoclinic).

**Decision criteria**:

| Region | Color | Criteria | Physical meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Stable** | 🟩 Green | ratio $\ge 0.615$ and mismatch $< 6.5$ | ideal cubic phase |
| **Metastable** | 🟨 Yellow | ratio $\ge 0.605$ | tetragonal/cubic mixed phase (high-conductivity potential) |
| **Unstable** | 🟥 Red | ratio too low or mismatch too high | monoclinic phase or phase-separation risk |

## 5. Discussion

### 5.1 Phase-stability prediction
With a cation/anion ratio of 0.6115, the material falls into the **metastable region**.

* **Code output**: `METASTABLE (Tetragonal/Cubic Mixed - High Conductivity)`
* **Physical meaning**: although the ratio is slightly below the ideal cubic-stability threshold (0.615), it remains within the metastable high-conductivity interval. This suggests the material may form a tetragonal phase or a tetragonal/cubic mixed phase. In practice, such metastable materials often show excellent ionic conductivity and improved mechanical toughness (compared with purely cubic phase).

* **Lattice constant**: estimated as **5.1308 Å** (computed by linear interpolation from ionic radii; the exact value in the file is 5.13076 Å).
* **Included**: element types (Zr, O, Sc, Mg) and cubic lattice parameters.
* ⚠️ **Missing**:

* **Recommendation**: use this file as a template for lattice parameters and composition only. For formal DFT, construct a full supercell with dopant occupations and complete coordinates using tools such as pymatgen, VESTA, or SQS methods.

## 7. Conclusion
This experiment indicates that the AI-recommended **Sc–Mg co-doped zirconia recipe** is a **metastable, high-conductivity-potential material** under Shannon-ionic-radius empirical criteria. The radius mismatch is low (~1.15 pm), suggesting manageable structural risk.

> ⚠️ Two limitations should be noted:

> 2. The generated POSCAR is a structural stub lacking atom counts and full coordinates; a proper supercell must be built before formal DFT calculations.
